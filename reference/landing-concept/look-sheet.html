MajorCycle landing film — the look

Stills only. Nothing here moves yet. Judge the feel: the investor, the company tiles, the machine, the colours, and one finished frame of scene 1 sitting on the real page background.

1 · Scene 1, finished frame, on the landing page

The film sits in one rounded frame on the site's existing light background. The header, button and disclaimer are the site's own. Night scenes use the brand's navy, so the dark frame reads as part of the site, not a different one.

Deliberately neither a man nor a woman. The head is a rounded square, the same shape as our logo and the company tiles, so the whole film is built from one shape. Hoodie in brand blue.

3 · The company tiles

One small character per company. Grey until checked. They pick up a stamp, a glow and finally a score and a colour as they pass through the machine.

4 · The MajorCycle machine

Navy, with our mark. Tiles go in the funnel, pass three windows (one per check), and roll out of the chute in order.
