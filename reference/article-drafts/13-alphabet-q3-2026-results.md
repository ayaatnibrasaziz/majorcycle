## Body

### The short version

Alphabet reported its results for the three months to 30 September after the US market closed on
28 October 2026. In short:

- **Revenue:** $127.6 billion, up 25% from $102.3 billion a year earlier.
- **Google Cloud:** revenue of $30.7 billion, up 103%, and operating income of $11.1 billion,
  up from $3.6 billion.
- **Profit:** operating income of $40.6 billion, up 30%. Net income was $26.5 billion, down 24%,
  because of an $8.7 billion loss on shares Alphabet holds.
- **Earnings per share:** $2.12, down from $2.87.
- **Spending:** $51.4 billion on property and equipment, against $24.0 billion a year earlier.
- **Free cash flow:** negative $9.1 billion, after negative $5.9 billion the quarter before.

All figures are in US dollars.

### The quarter against a year earlier

| | Third quarter 2026 | A year earlier | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | $127.6B | $102.3B | +24.7% |
| Google Search & other | $63.4B | $56.6B | +12.0% |
| YouTube ads | $11.2B | $10.3B | +9.2% |
| Google subscriptions, platforms and devices | $14.3B | $12.9B | +11.0% |
| Google Cloud | $30.7B | $15.2B | +102.6% |
| Operating income | $40.6B | $31.2B | +29.9% |
| Net income | $26.5B | $35.0B | −24.3% |
| Diluted earnings per share | $2.12 | $2.87 | |

Google Cloud was the engine. Alphabet says sales of its TPU systems "were also a meaningful
contributor to revenue growth", and Cloud's operating income more than tripled. Chief executive
Sundar Pichai said Alphabet is "now on a half-trillion dollar revenue run rate", and that the
Gemini app "has reached one billion users".

### Why profit fell

The fall in net income came from below the operating line. A year earlier, gains on the shares
Alphabet holds in other companies added $10.7 billion. This quarter they lost $8.7 billion, and
Alphabet says that loss cut its net income by $6.7 billion after tax. Operating income, which
leaves those investments out, rose 30%.

### Where the money is going

| | Third quarter 2026 | A year earlier |
|---|---|---|
| Cash from operations | $42.3B | $48.4B |
| Spent on property and equipment | $51.4B | $24.0B |
| Free cash flow | −$9.1B | $24.5B |

Alphabet defines free cash flow as cash from operations less capital spending. Spending rose 114%
and for the second quarter in a row it was more than the business brought in.

To pay for it, Alphabet raised $28.7 billion from new bonds and sold $12.4 billion of new shares
through its at-the-market program. It bought back no shares this quarter, against $11.5 billion a
year earlier.

### How the shares reacted

Alphabet's Class A shares closed at $324.89 on 28 October, before the results came out. In after-hours trading, at 6:47pm New York time, they were at $292.90, down 9.8%. That is 27.3% below their record close. After-hours trading is thin, and the price can move a long way before the next day's close, so this is the reaction at that moment, not a final one.

On 27 October, the day before the results, Alphabet's Class A shares closed at $320.58. That was:

- **20.4% below their record close** of $402.62 on 13 May 2026,
- **2.4% higher than at the start of 2026**, when they stood at $313.00.

### How Alphabet shares usually react to results

We measured all 43 regular results releases from February 2016 to July 2026, comparing the close
on the day after with the close on the day Alphabet reported:

- The middle move, up or down, was **3.8%**.
- 17 of the 43 moved by 5% or more, and one by 10% or more.
- The largest fall was **9.5%** in October 2023.
- The largest rise was **10.2%** in April 2024.
- The shares rose after 23 of the 43 and fell after 20.

### How far Alphabet has fallen before

Counting dividends, Alphabet has fallen 20% or more nine times since it listed in August 2004,
counting the current fall. These are measured from a record close to the lowest close before the
shares got back to it:

| Record close | Lowest point | Fall | Back at the record |
|---|---|---|---|
| January 2006 | March 2006 | −28.5% | October 2006 |
| November 2007 | November 2008 | −65.3% | September 2012 |
| July 2018 | December 2018 | −23.4% | April 2019 |
| February 2020 | March 2020 | −30.9% | July 2020 |
| November 2021 | November 2022 | −44.3% | January 2024 |
| July 2024 | September 2024 | −22.1% | December 2024 |
| February 2025 | April 2025 | −29.8% | August 2025 |
| February 2026 | March 2026 | −20.4% | April 2026 |
| May 2026 | October 2026 (deepest so far, before these results) | −21.2% | Not yet |

The first eight all got back to their record. The quickest was back about a month after its low,
in April 2026; the slowest, after the 2008 fall, took almost four years. That is a record of what
happened, not a guide to what will.

Measured on our stock page's setting, Alphabet normally falls about 15.8% below its one-year high
before turning. On 27 October it was 21.4% below, past its normal fall.

### What comes next

- **Google Cloud.** Whether growth near 100% holds.
- **Spending and cash.** Whether spending keeps running ahead of cash from operations.
- **Investments.** Gains and losses on the shares Alphabet holds can move its net income in
  either direction from quarter to quarter.
