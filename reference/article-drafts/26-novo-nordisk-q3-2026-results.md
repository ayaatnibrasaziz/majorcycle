## Body

### The short version

Novo Nordisk, the Danish company that makes the diabetes and weight-loss medicines Ozempic and Wegovy, reported its
results for the three months to 30 September 2026 on 4 November. In short:

- **Sales:** DKK 79,195 million, up 4% at constant exchange rates, which leave out the effect of currency moves,
  and up 6% in Danish kroner.
- **The Wegovy pill:** sales of DKK 5,394 million in the quarter.
- **Profit:** adjusted operating profit of DKK 31,720 million, up 27% at constant exchange rates. Reported
  operating profit fell 9%, after DKK 8.7 billion of impairment charges.
- **The outlook:** sales and adjusted operating profit for 2026 now expected to change by between −2% and +2% at
  constant exchange rates, up from 0% to −6% in August.
- **The shares:** Novo's US-listed shares rose 5.0% on 4 November, to $42.18.

### The quarter

| | Jul–Sep 2026, DKK | Growth in DKK | Growth at constant exchange rates |
|---|---|---|---|
| Net sales | 79,195M | +6% | +4% |
| Adjusted operating profit | 31,720M | +34% | +27% |
| Operating profit | 23,011M | −3% | −9% |

Novo said adjusted operating profit was helped by "a favourable comparison base following DKK 9 billion of
company-wide transformation costs recognised in Q3 2025". The reported figure fell because of DKK 8.7 billion of
non-cash impairment charges: DKK 4.6 billion for ziltivekimab, a drug in development, and DKK 4.1 billion for
"right-sizing of manufacturing network". The ziltivekimab charge followed two late-stage trials, ZEUS and HERMES,
which Novo said "did not meet the primary endpoint".

### Its medicines

| Medicine | Jul–Sep 2026, DKK |
|---|---|
| Ozempic | 29,706M |
| Wegovy, injectable | 20,407M |
| Wegovy pill | 5,394M |
| Ozempic pill and Rybelsus | 4,938M |

Novo said the Wegovy pill had been launched in eight markets, and that the Wegovy franchise had "more than 50%
share in the branded obesity market" in the US.

### Where the sales came from

| Jul–Sep 2026 | DKK | Growth at constant exchange rates |
|---|---|---|
| US Operations | 41,880M | +1% |
| International Operations | 37,315M | +8% |
| Obesity medicines | 26,146M | +22% |
| Diabetes medicines | 47,545M | −5% |

So the growth came from outside the US and from obesity. Novo said sales grew because it sold more of its GLP-1
medicines, "partly offset by lower realised prices", the prices it actually receives after discounts.

It also said the Ozempic pill, launched in the US in May, was off to an encouraging start: weekly new prescriptions
were "currently up around 100% compared with pre-launch levels under the Rybelsus" brand.

### Against what analysts expected

Before the results, analysts had expected sales of DKK 73.6 billion and adjusted operating profit of
DKK 28.6 billion, according to FactSet figures reported by CNBC. Novo reported DKK 79,195 million and
DKK 31,720 million.

### The outlook

| 2026, at constant exchange rates | In August | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Adjusted sales growth | 0% to −6% | −2% to +2% |
| Adjusted operating profit growth | 0% to −6% | −2% to +2% |

Novo said the higher outlook was "driven by increased expectations for GLP-1 product sales", the class of medicines
Ozempic and Wegovy belong to.

Chief executive Mike Doustdar said: "We delivered a stronger-than-expected third quarter and are raising our
full-year outlook for adjusted sales and adjusted operating profit."
He added: "We are particularly pleased to see both US Operations and International Operations deliver sales growth
this quarter."

### Profit and cash

Novo's adjusted gross margin was 82.1%. Its net profit fell 16% to DKK 16,740 million, after the impairment charges.
In the quarter, its free cash flow was DKK 28,483 million, down 13%.

Over the first nine months of 2026, Novo generated DKK 121.0 billion of cash from its operations and returned
DKK 61.9 billion to shareholders: DKK 51.9 billion in dividends and DKK 10.0 billion in share buybacks.

Mike Doustdar also said: "Our focus is on sustaining this progress through disciplined execution and broader access
to our medicines."

These are Novo's views of its own business, not ours.

### How the shares reacted

Novo's US-listed shares closed at $40.19 on 3 November. On 4 November, the day of the results, they closed at
$42.18, up 5.0%. The S&P 500 rose 1.6% the same day.

On 5 November, when the Nasdaq Composite fell 5.5% and the S&P 500 4.4%, the shares fell back to $40.30.

Counting dividends, that leaves them 70.3% below their record close in June 2024.

### How Novo shares usually react to results

We measured all 34 of Novo's results from May 2018 to August 2026, comparing the US-listed shares' close on the day
of each announcement with the close the day before.

- The middle move, up or down, was **3.6%**.
- Nine of the 34 moved the shares 5% or more, and two 10% or more.
- The largest fall was **14.6%** in February 2026.
- The largest rise was **6.4%** in November 2022.
- The shares rose on 18 of the 34 days and fell on 16.

So this was a bigger move than usual: only nine of the 34 earlier results moved the shares more.

### How far Novo has fallen before

Novo's US-listed shares have fallen 40% or more six times since 1981, the start of our price history, counting this
one. These are measured from a record close to the lowest close before the shares got back to it, counting
dividends:

| Record close | Lowest point | Fall | Back at the record |
|---|---|---|---|
| September 1983 | October 1987 | −71.3% | February 1991 |
| May 1998 | March 1999 | −42.9% | May 2000 |
| August 2001 | August 2002 | −51.3% | February 2004 |
| April 2008 | April 2009 | −41.3% | February 2010 |
| August 2015 | November 2016 | −46.1% | January 2018 |
| June 2024 | March 2026 | −74.7% | Not yet |

The five earlier falls all got back to their record, the longest after more than seven years. That is a record of
what happened, not a guide to what will.

### Legal matters

Novo faces several class actions in the US over an IT security incident it disclosed on 11 June 2026. On 9
September, at the plaintiffs' request, the court dismissed them without prejudice, which means they can be filed
again.

Separately, on 27 August 2026 a US appeals court upheld the dismissal of a lawsuit that compounding businesses
brought against the US Food and Drug Administration over its decision that a shortage of semaglutide injections, the
medicine in Ozempic and Wegovy, had ended. Novo had joined the case to oppose them.

Novo said it does not expect either matter to have a material impact on its financial position, operating profit or
cash flow.

### What comes next

- **The outlook.** Whether sales for 2026 land within the −2% to +2% Novo now expects.
- **The Wegovy pill.** Whether its sales keep growing as it launches in more markets.
- **CagriSema.** Novo is preparing for its US launch in early 2027, subject to approval. In the REDEFINE 9 trial it
  gave weight loss of 21% at week 68, Novo said. In another trial, REIMAGINE 5, it said CagriSema gave weight loss
  of 12.4% against 9.1% for tirzepatide 5 mg, a rival medicine, in adults with type 2 diabetes and obesity.

### What this cannot tell you

These are results for one quarter. Forecasts are Novo's own, or analysts' as reported, not ours. The share-price
history is a record of what happened before, not a prediction. This article is general information and does not
take into account your objectives, financial situation or needs.
